# AI-Powered Traffic Congestion and Road Accident Risk Prediction & Analytics System

## Project Overview
This Data Science capstone analyzes historical road crashes, traffic congestion patterns, and geographic hotspots in the Dallas-Fort Worth area of Texas.

## Problem Statement
Road accidents are a major public safety concern. Their patterns may be associated with time, weather, roadway characteristics, traffic conditions, and location. This project will analyze available historical crash data, identify high-crash locations, evaluate suitable machine learning models, and present findings in an interactive dashboard.

## Objectives
1. Collect and inspect historical crash data.
2. Clean and prepare the data for analysis.
3. Explore crash patterns by time, severity, weather, and location.
4. Identify historical accident hotspots.
5. Train and evaluate appropriate machine learning models.
6. Explain model predictions using feature importance and, where suitable, SHAP.
7. Build an interactive analytics dashboard.

## Research Questions
1. Which temporal, environmental, and roadway factors are associated with recorded crashes?
2. Which locations have the highest historical concentrations of crashes?
3. How well do selected machine learning models perform against a baseline?
4. Which features contribute most to model predictions?

## Initial Scope
- Geography: Dallas and Tarrant Counties, Texas.
- Historical period: 2021-2025, subject to data availability.
- Primary data: Historical road crash records.
- Optional data: Weather, traffic, and geographic information, where compatible data is available.

## Technology Stack

## Project Limitations
Historical crash records alone do not establish the probability of a crash occurring at every road location and time. Reliable crash-occurrence prediction requires appropriate non-crash observations and compatible exposure or traffic data. Historical hotspot analysis and crash-severity prediction will be distinguished from future crash-occurrence prediction.
